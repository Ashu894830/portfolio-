04 / How I work

A repeatable growth loop.

Good performance marketing is a process: understand the goal, build a test, measure honestly and improve what comes next.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the business goal, offer, customer, budget and success metric.

STEP 02

Build

Structure the campaign, message, audience, landing page and tracking.

STEP 03

Measure

Review the data in context and separate signal from noise.

STEP 04

Improve

Document learnings, prioritise experiments and iterate responsibly.
